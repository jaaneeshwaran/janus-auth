Six lifecycle signals.

Each mode isolates one defect. The adjudicator reads canaries, post-probe state, and the surviving-grant control.

  • BOLA read leak

    Canary present after revocation.

  • Silent write side effect

    State mutated behind a 403.

  • Stale group cache

    Pending member still authorized.

  • Former owner retention

    Former owner survives transfer.

  • Broken delegation cascade

    Delegation outlives its parent.

  • Over-revocation regression

    Branch B denied despite a surviving grant.

Measured, not asserted.
